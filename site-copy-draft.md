*Typical length: 1 week · Flat fee*

---

### AI Readiness Audit + Roadmap
*The core engagement*

A 3-week engagement that goes deeper: stakeholder interviews, infrastructure review, and a concrete roadmap — not a deck full of observations, but a sequenced action plan with real priorities. Covers data governance, EU AI Act compliance (for European clients), and where LLM or ML implementation is actually ready to deliver value vs. where it's still hype.

Useful if: you're ready to move on AI but don't want to make expensive mistakes, or your teams are pulling in different directions and you need alignment before execution.

*Typical length: 3 weeks · Flat fee*

---

### AI Transformation Retainer
*The long game*

An ongoing monthly engagement for companies that need a senior technical voice embedded in their decisions — architecture choices, team guidance, vendor evaluation, keeping ML initiatives honest between sprints. Think of it as a fractional CDO without the overhead of a full hire.

Useful if: you're post-audit and executing, or you're a founder who needs someone to hold the technical strategy while you focus on the business.

*Ongoing · Monthly · 1–2 clients at a time*

# STRATEGIC NOTES

## The core differentiator (from prior research session)
The line that no other data consultant in Valencia has: **"Your data problem is often a language problem."** This should appear early — either in the hero or in the first line of About. It earns attention from non-technical executives because it reframes the problem in terms they recognize, and it signals immediately that you're not a commodity technical hire.

## Offer pyramid logic
The three engagement tiers map to a sales funnel:
- **Infrastructure Review** (entry) → low friction, fast yes, creates the upsell conversation
- **AI Readiness Audit** (middle) → bread and butter, repeatable, referrable, €4–8k range
- **Transformation Retainer** (top) → where the real relationship lives, €5–10k/month, 1–2 max

No pricing on the page. Ranges live in the sales conversation.

## Spain-specific moat
EU AI Act compliance is becoming a real consulting category. Add it explicitly to the Audit description (done above). Most data engineers ignore it; you understanding the governance layer makes you the safe choice for European SMBs. This is worth making more prominent over time as enforcement ramps up.

## Current ML focus
The site should signal RAG/LLM implementation competence, not just "ML" abstractly — because that's what clients are actually buying right now. The phrase "LLM and RAG systems" appears in Sections 2 and 3. Update as your stack evolves.

## Substack / newsletter
